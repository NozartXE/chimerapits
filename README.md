CHIMERA PITS — splice a beast, watch it fight, graft it stranger. Your beast battles the wild on its own in a pit arena; your strategy is its body — and your hand stays in the fight: click any wild beast to MARK it and your beast hunts that one until it falls. Win a bout and the yard offers 3 grafts — a serpent head that spits venom, gull wings, a scorpion tail, a twin brain — and the beast that walks into the next bout is visibly, mechanically a different animal. And the pit itself reconfigures too: pit moods (DOWNPOUR, DEEP MIRE, BITING SWARM, LONG SHADOWS, WHITE HEAT) bend projectile speed, chain windows, knockback and venom for both sides. 36 grafts, 7 tag synergies, 9 wild kinds, bruiser bouts, and at bout 9 the three-phase APEX, THE UNSPLICED. Applause prods, 3 stances, style chains, 3 difficulties, an endless WILD mode, 9 feats and an almanac. Deterministic: the fixed-step simulation replays identically from a seed, in the browser and headlessly in Node.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts   # then open http://localhost:8080
```
